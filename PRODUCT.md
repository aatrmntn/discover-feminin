# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS in a single file, hosted on GitHub Pages.

## Users

New patients choosing a center for women's health in Vienna. Typically on a phone, often searching around a life event (first gynecologist visit, Kinderwunsch, pregnancy, a new baby, menopause), deciding whether feminin is the right place and how to get an appointment.

## Product Purpose

A discovery flow for feminin.at, not a homepage: a first-time visitor describes her concern or answers a few questions about life phase, health issues and concerns, and gets a clear recommendation of service, people, billing and how to book. Success: she finds her topic, sees the people behind it, and calls or writes for an appointment.

## Positioning

feminin is a center for Frau* Mutter* Kind in Wien-Döbling where gynecology, prenatal diagnostics, midwives, pediatrics, psychotherapy, physiotherapy, dermatology, surgery, nutrition and wellbeing (femdrop, femfit, feminin.aesthetic) sit under one roof. The holistic, all-life-phases setup is the claim a single-specialty practice cannot copy.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from search or a shared link, mostly mobile. Booking happens by phone (+43 1 394 01 08), email (ordination@feminin.at) or the existing online booking. Address Nusswaldgasse 5, 1190 Wien. Hours Mo 7 to 19, Di to Do 8 to 19, Fr 7 to 14.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Only Dr. Patrik Horak treats Kassenpatient:innen; all others are Wahlärzt:innen, invoices are forwarded to the insurer.
- 100 EUR no-show fee if not cancelled 24 hours ahead.
- No online slot data available to this prototype; the action is call or email.
- The mapping of services to life phases is a proposal, to be confirmed by the center.

## Brand Commitments

Name "feminin", tagline "Frau* Mutter* Kind", gender asterisk in "Frauen*". Visual identity from feminin.at as captured in the provided PDFs: rust brown type, clay circle logo, organic blob-shaped photography, fine line uterus illustration, outlined square buttons. Formal "Sie" address.

## Evidence on Hand

Team list, service list, founder texts, photos (founders, reception, yoga) from the two PDF exports of feminin.at. No testimonials, ratings, patient numbers or prices beyond the no-show fee; none may be invented.

## Product Principles

- Start from her situation, not from the org chart.
- Show people, not departments.
- Booking is always one tap away.
- Calm and warm, never clinical, never salesy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Sensitive topics (Schwangerschaftsabbruch, mental health) are presented neutrally and discreetly. WCAG AA contrast, keyboard operable, reduced motion respected.
