Scope: index.html, a discovery flow (not a homepage; feminin.at already has one). Mode: Operate.
Audience: new patients unsure what they need. Task: describe a concern or answer three questions, get a clear recommendation, book it.
Content: real services and team from feminin.at; concern vocabulary authored in patients' own words; Kasse rule; Austrian emergency numbers 144 and 142.
Constraints: no invented claims; booking via phone or prefilled email; sensitive topics neutral.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A calm, one-question-at-a-time consultation that starts from the visitor's own words and ends with one clear recommendation and a way to book. Refuses the homepage, the service catalogue grid, and decorative set pieces.

OWN-WORLD: feminin.at's identity: white ground, rust type and 2px rust rules, clay logo circle, square outlined controls, Belleza for questions and service names, Urbanist for everything else, one soft sand blob as the only ornament. Selected states fill sand with a rust check.

STORY: She types or taps what worries her, sees it understood in her words, learns which service and which people fit and how it is billed, and calls or sends a prefilled email.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Logo and phone top. One large question, "Was beschäftigt Sie?", a large search field directly beneath with live suggestions, common concerns as quick chips, and one clear secondary path, "Schritt für Schritt herausfinden".

FORM: Guided consultation (own-world, brief-pinned identity; seed key: none, pinned).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
